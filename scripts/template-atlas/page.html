How a composition is built

CompositionOne clinical document

A visit note or an order, saved in a patient's EHR. Each new save of it is a new version.

ContextWho, when, where

Author, start time, setting, facility. Sent once as ctx/… fields in FLAT format.

EntryClinical statement

An archetype such as Blood pressure (observation), Problem/Diagnosis (evaluation) or Medication order (instruction).

ElementSingle value

A quantity with unit, a coded term (ICD-10, ATC), text or a date. Groups of elements are clusters.
